## Operation: How the Mat Uses Power

When powered, the mat may illuminate or power USB ports, features that rely on a steady voltage supply and can drain a battery if left on while the car is off. Since the exact power draw isn’t disclosed, you should inquire about wattage and whether the mat includes an auto‑shutoff timer.

## Installation and Setup Steps

Installation typically involves positioning the mat, routing the power cable to a suitable outlet, and securing any clips or adhesives; without a detailed guide, you should request the installation manual or a video walkthrough to avoid damaging interior trim.

## Maintenance and Longevity

Ask whether the manufacturer offers a replacement warranty for wear or material defects, and check if the power components are sealed against moisture, which is important for cars that experience frequent rain or humidity in city environments.
