Setup and Maintenance Tips for Each Tool

Begin with the soldering iron by confirming the 110 V supply matches local outlets, setting the temperature within the advertised range, and cleaning the tip after each use. For the paint set, rinse brushes in lukewarm water, dry them gently, and reseal each bottle to prevent drying.

For the cutting mat, lay it flat on a stable surface, ensure the grid aligns with the cutter’s blade, and wipe the surface after each session. Storing the mat folded protects its edges, and occasional rotation spreads wear, keeping the self‑healing layer effective over time.
