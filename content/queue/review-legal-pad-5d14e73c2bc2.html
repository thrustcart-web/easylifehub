Understanding Paper Weight and Ink Bleed

Paper weight is the primary factor determining if your fountain pens, markers, or highlighters will show through to the reverse side. Manufacturers measure this in pounds or grams per square meter, but these specifications are not always prominently listed on the packaging.

You should verify the thickness requirements for your preferred pens before committing to a pad. If you intend to use heavy-flow ink, examine the manufacturer documentation for specific references to paper fiber density or acid-free quality to minimize bleed-through across the 70 sheets.

Evaluating Binding and Page Removal

The Ampad Gold Fibre Classic utilizes a wirebound design, which keeps pages secure while allowing the pad to lie flat when open. This is essential for a planning system where you need to reference previous entries while documenting new tasks on the current page.

Check that the wire coil is sufficiently coated to prevent snagging on your clothing or storage sleeves. Consider how often you need to remove pages, as the perforation quality dictates how cleanly you can archive your notes after the list is complete.

Selecting the Correct Ruling for Daily Lists

Legal ruling provides a standard horizontal structure that works well for chronological task lists. However, some bullet journalers prefer grids or dots for more complex layouts, so you must verify the interior ruling before purchase.

The standard 8-1/2 by 11-3/4 inch size offers significant writing surface, which supports long-form logs. Ensure this footprint fits your workspace or bag, as larger pads provide more lines but require more desk clearance during active planning sessions.
