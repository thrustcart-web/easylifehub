Maintaining Brushes After Heavy Priming

Primer dries hard. If it is not removed correctly, it can stiffen the bristles permanently. After using these brushes for surface priming, you must clean them thoroughly. Soaking in warm water and a neutral soap helps loosen the dried paint from the ferrule.

Do not leave dirty brushes in a cup of water, as the contamination spreads. Dry them with the bristles pointing down to prevent water from entering the ferrule, which can cause the bristles to fall out. Proper care extends the life of the set, which is particularly important if you are buying a set of twelve for ongoing use.
