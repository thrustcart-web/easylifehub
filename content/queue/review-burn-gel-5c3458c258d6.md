## Why Expiration Dates Directly Affect First Aid Gel Safety

Burn gel formulas degrade over time, which alters their physical texture, moisture level, and active ingredient concentration. Fresh gel maintains its intended consistency to cover minor cuts, scrapes, and burns evenly without separation or drying out.

Sealed tubes shield active ingredients from atmospheric exposure, but packaging materials can slowly degrade over several years. Verifying the stamped expiration date on the tube ensures the physical barrier remains completely intact and free from external contamination.

## How Should Families Store Burn Gel in Household Kits?

Storing burn gel in a cool, dry cabinet prevents ambient heat from breaking down the topical gel formulation. Temperature swings inside vehicle glove compartments, hot garages, or sunlit bathrooms can accelerate product breakdown and alter fluid consistency.

Keep the topical gel in its original outer box or tube so the printed batch number and expiration date remain easily legible. Storing first-aid supplies in accessible, climate-controlled locations keeps them ready for sudden household needs.
