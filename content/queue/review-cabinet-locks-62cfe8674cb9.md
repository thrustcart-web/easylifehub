## How Width Range Impacts Cabinet Latch Performance

Width options determine whether a safety latch can bridge double doors or wrap around cabinet corners cleanly. Fixed-length latches require exact spacing between mounting points to function correctly. Measuring the clearance between cabinet handles and door frames reveals if a flexible span is required for secure engagement.

If a latch lacks sufficient reach, tension pulls against the mounting pads during everyday operation. Excess tension risks pulling standard adhesive bonds away from painted or laminated surfaces. Verifying the maximum span listed by the manufacturer prevents premature wear on non-drilling safety fasteners.

## Surface Preparation and Maintenance Checks

Proper surface preparation prevents adhesive failure on frequently accessed kitchen and bathroom cabinets. Removing oil, grease, and dust from the mounting target ensures maximum adhesive bonding. Regular inspection keeps safety hardware working reliably over months of daily access.

Periodically check the latch tension and adhesive pad alignment to confirm the lock remains firmly attached. Household cleaners should be applied around, rather than directly onto, adhesive bases to preserve the bond strength. When move-out time arrives, carefully warming the adhesive pad allows removal without stripping base paint.
