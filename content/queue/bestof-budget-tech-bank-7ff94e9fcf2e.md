## Key Compatibility and Power Considerations Before Purchasing

Cable reach and power delivery dictate where hardware can be installed. Desk accessories operating via USB require accessible ports or nearby wall adapters to maintain constant power. Verify port availability on your current computer or multi-port hub before adding multiple powered accessories.

Physical mounting surfaces must also match equipment attachment designs. Monitor edges, thin laptop lids, and small desks each present distinct physical tolerances that determine whether clip-on lights or mini tripods remain secure during daily operation.
