## Installation and Setup Process

Start by mapping your garden rows and measuring the distance between water outlets and plant zones. Cut the tubing to match those measurements, then snap the quick‑connect fittings together as indicated in the kit’s instruction sheet.

## Maintenance and Longevity

Regularly inspect emitters for mineral buildup, especially if you have hard water. Cleaning a clogged emitter usually involves removing it and rinsing with water or a gentle brush.

When the season ends, drain the tubing and store it in a dry location. This practice extends the life of the polyethylene and prevents mold growth. Verify that any stored sections are kept away from direct heat sources.
