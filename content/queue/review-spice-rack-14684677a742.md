## Stackability and Space Efficiency

A key feature highlighted is stackability, meaning the two units can be placed one on top of the other to double vertical storage. This can be useful when ceiling height is available but wall width is limited.

Before relying on stackability, confirm the mounting hardware can support the combined weight of both racks and filled spice jars. Verify the manufacturer’s instructions or test the load capacity on a sample wall.

## Wall‑Mount Installation Process

Installation requires drilling pilot holes for the supplied screws and anchors. The package includes the hardware, but the exact size of the anchors is not detailed, so check that they match your wall type—drywall, plaster, or tile.

Align the rack level before securing it, as the aluminum frame does not allow for post‑installation adjustment. Use a spirit level to avoid a slanted shelf that could cause jars to roll.

## Operation and Accessibility

Spice jars sit directly on the aluminum shelf without additional brackets, providing quick access when you need a seasoning. The open design also keeps labels visible, reducing the time spent searching.

Because there are no side walls, heavier jars may shift if the rack is tilted. Consider using a non‑slip mat or securing jars with a thin silicone ring if you anticipate frequent movement.

## Cleaning and Maintenance Guidelines

Aluminum surfaces can be wiped with a damp cloth and mild detergent; avoid abrasive cleaners that could wear the anodized coating.

Periodically inspect the mounting screws for loosening, especially after adding or removing several heavy jars. Tighten as needed to maintain a secure attachment.

## Conclusion

When evaluating the KES spice rack, verify material durability, exact dimensions, mounting compatibility, and the ability to stack securely before purchase.
