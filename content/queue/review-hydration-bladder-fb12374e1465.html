Capacity Options and Trade‑offs

The listed product provides three volume choices: 1.5 L, 2 L, and 3 L. Smaller capacities keep the pack light and sit low in the frame, while larger sizes reduce refill stops but add weight. Decide how many hours you expect to be on the trail between water sources and choose accordingly.
